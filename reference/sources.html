Canonical sources and path conventions

The primary reference is the inspected PS3 EU full release. Extracted resources use the non-flattened Full view. Other releases and platforms supplement this baseline; differences must be attributed to their source.

Source identities and roots

Paths use forward slashes and are relative to the named root, never a contributor's drive. Source identifiers describe the inspected local reference, not every build sold in that region.

Path conventions

  • ps3-eu-original:PS3_GAME/USRDIR/... starts at the directory containing PS3_GAME.
  • ps3-eu-extracted:A2_Frontend/... starts at PS3-EU-Full-audio_reorg-isRenamed.
  • ps3-eu-flattened:A2_Frontend/... starts at PS3-EU-FullFlattened-audio_reorg-isRenamed.
  • A page or inventory must declare its source and path namespace. Historical mixed inventories are explicitly marked as awaiting path-level reconciliation; they must not be interpreted as canonical paths.
  • New examples use the canonical extracted namespace. Retained flattened measurements keep their original provenance until checked against Full. Display names and aliases are described in the naming reference.

Supplementary observations

PS3 US, PS3 demo and Xbox 360 observations must state platform, region, release identity when known, and path root. Historical Xbox NTSC inventories retain that label; they are not silently relabeled as the local Xbox EU set. Matching extensions can share a page with labeled variant sections. Xbox-only extensions remain in an xbox subfolder at the corresponding container location.

Historical research and migration

Existing byte layouts, measurements and sample dumps are retained. Source notices distinguish legacy mixed paths from new canonical references. Unresolved paths, archive membership and whole-build identity remain research tasks, not assumed equivalences. Tool behavior is supporting evidence and must be distinguished from observed original-game behavior.
