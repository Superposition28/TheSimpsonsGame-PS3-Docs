Evidence and limitations

The previous page described ALB/CTB/SNU relationships rather than an AUB layout. Those relationships now live in the format relationship guide; they are not evidence of the AUB format itself.

Detailed field tables and retained samples are research evidence. Implementation-derived observations must remain attributed to their tools; tentative, observed and contextual relationships do not establish complete original runtime behavior. Offsets in the added tables are zero-based; range ends are exclusive unless stated otherwise.

The reference inventory lists two AUB files. Their binary structure, stable signature and runtime role have not been established by the current notes or registered operations.

Identification and structure

Retain the original filename, archive path, size and leading bytes before classifying a sample. No validated header, record stride, pointer base or compression rule is available for AUB. The suffix alone is not evidence that it shares the ALB or CTB layout.
