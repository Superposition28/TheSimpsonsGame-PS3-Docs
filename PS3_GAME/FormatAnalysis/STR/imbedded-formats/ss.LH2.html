Evidence and limitations

The inventory below was read from the local EU flattened dataset. It is not a universal regional inventory. File paths retain their normalization hashes; the six-hex suffix is not an LH2 StringID.

Detailed field tables and retained samples are research evidence. Implementation-derived observations must remain attributed to their tools; tentative, observed and contextual relationships do not establish complete original runtime behavior. Offsets in the added tables are zero-based; range ends are exclusive unless stated otherwise.

This variant uses the shared LH2 binary layout. The observed simpsons_global.ss.LH2 data contains internal labels such as CREDITS_IntEngineers_1. Operation 10 uses it to name cues and derive speaker labels; it is not emitted as a fifth subtitle language.

The original EU dialogue audit also validates five-column unsuffixed LH2 tables (English, French, Italian, Spanish, internal label). Individual SNU dialogue links resolve through ALB/SMB to those rows; this global suffix inventory is not the complete dialogue-table inventory.

Variant identification

Match the full .ss.LH2 compound suffix, allowing the six-hex normalization hash before it. Do not confuse language suffixes with arbitrary unsuffixed LH2 tables; column meaning is a content-level property.
