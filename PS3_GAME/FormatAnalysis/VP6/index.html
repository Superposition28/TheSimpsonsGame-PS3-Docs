Evidence and limitations

The registered FFmpeg conversion exports OGV. It does not resolve LH2 text, create subtitle cues or prove equivalence of PS3 and Xbox 360 movies. Subtitle pairing is a separate operation.

Operation coverage is grounded in the module manifest and its referenced implementations. Detailed field tables and retained samples below/above are the existing research evidence; labels such as tentative, observed and context must not be read as a complete runtime implementation. Offsets in the added tables are zero-based; range ends are exclusive unless stated otherwise.

The game stores VP6-encoded movies in an EA chunk container that can include movie audio. The file extension names the video codec family rather than describing subtitle text or a bitmap font.

Identification and observed structure

Observed movie chunks and markers; not a complete chunk schema
Marker Role Evidence limit
SCHl Audio header in analyzed movies. Internal fields depend on the EA audio header variant.
SHxx Language-tagged audio header family described in the earlier research. Check the individual movie; do not infer multiple languages from the extension.
MVhd, vp60 Movie header and VP6 codec marker. Resolution, frame rate and chunk offsets must be read per file.
SCDl, SCCl, MV0K Audio/video-associated data markers recorded in the research. The full interleaving and size-field schema is not specified here.

Preserve four-character chunk IDs as literal bytes. Numeric byte order is a separate question: a PS3 source does not make every chunk-size field big-endian. Use a decoder/probe and validate each chunk boundary against the actual file length.

Current EU header check: all 172 local VP6 files begin with literal SCHl. The next four bytes decode as a little-endian chunk size of 40 (73 files) or 44 (99 files). This establishes that field for this corpus, not the byte order of every payload field or every EA movie variant.

Conversion and playback

Operation 13 delegates .vp6 to .ogv conversion to FFmpeg with replacement enabled. Audio decoding alone through vgmstream is not video conversion. Inspect the output streams when a movie has multiple tracks; the manifest does not request exhaustive stream mapping.

Operation 16 can write external SRT/VTT companions beside either the original VP6 or converted OGV. Captions remain separate files: the registered video conversion does not burn text into frames or choose an INF font. Pairing works by movie stem and language directory, not by parsing a font or subtitle pointer out of the movie.

Variants and unresolved fields

The original examples were attractloop.vp6 and loc_igc01.vp6. The sf directory contains special-feature movies in the EU inventory. Logos, attract loops and feature clips may have no non-empty TOX timing table. Cross-platform similarity does not establish byte-identical PS3 and Xbox 360 movies, codecs or headers.

A complete chunk field map, authoritative per-field byte order, track-selection rules and integrity/checksum scheme remain undocumented. Common resolutions or sample rates should be treated as observations of a particular movie, not format constants.
