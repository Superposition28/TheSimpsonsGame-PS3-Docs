Collision research: bunny and zone01

Inspected on 2026-10-22. ps3-eu-extracted:L01_LandOfChocolate/loc/Challenge_Mode/Challenge_Mode_Design_str/build/PS3/pal_en/assets/shared/collision/npc_bunny.acs.PS3 is a 724-byte set of nine primitive records, not a triangle mesh. Its supplied flattened alias ends in npc_bunny_94c523.acs.PS3. The ACS specification records the exact offsets, scalar parameters and joint lookup.

The same original ps3-eu-original:PS3_GAME/USRDIR/loc/loc/Challenge_Mode/Challenge_Mode_Design.str contains npc_default BBN and bunny_dark LOD3/LOD5 visual models. All bunny ACS binding IDs resolve through BBN aliases to rig hashes, and all 29 BBN rig hashes occur in both models' HAnim node tables. This proves an identifier bridge; applying collision volumes still requires the correct poses, skinning map, dimensions and coordinates.

For level collision, ps3-eu-extracted:L01_LandOfChocolate/loc/zone01_str/build/PS3/pal_en/assets/environs/loc/loc/zone01/export/collision/collisionmodel1.hkt.PS3 was checked against the member in original PS3_GAME/USRDIR/loc/loc/zone01.str. Its Havok mesh arrays are the terrain-collision route. Exact archive/member identities and hashes are in the provenance ledger; full bounded corpus results are in the 401-file inventory and Havok audit.
